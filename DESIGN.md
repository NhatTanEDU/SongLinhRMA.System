# SOLICOM RMA Design System

This design system establishes the visual standards and brand identity guidelines for the SOLICOM RMA System.

## Visual Theme & Atmosphere
The design system reflects a professional, reliable, and modern industrial aesthetic. It focuses on clarity, high readability, and clean layouts suitable for administrative, technical, and sales order tracking.

## Color Palette & Roles
The primary brand colors represent SOLICOM's visual identity:
*   **SOLICOM Blue (`#0072BC`):** Primary branding color used for structural headers, primary action buttons, and general brand representation.
*   **SOLICOM Red (`#E51D24`):** Secondary accent color used for secondary highlights, destructive actions, and status alerts.
*   **SOLICOM Yellow (`#FFF200`):** Warning color used for pending, caution, or attention-required states.
*   **Background (`#f4f6f8`):** Off-white light gray background providing a clean backdrop with good contrast for dashboard cards.

## Component Stylings
*   **AppBar:** Uses `Primary` (`#0072BC`) background with white text and white outline buttons for a cohesive, branded interface.
*   **Sidebar Drawer:** Uses a clean white background with a subtle border separator (`#e0e0e0`) and contains the SOLICOM round logo.
*   **Cards/Containers:** Styled with soft borders and minimal elevation to present sales and technical details cleanly.
